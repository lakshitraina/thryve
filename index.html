SAFARNAMA '26

"GOOD PEOPLE • GREAT PLACES • UNWIND TOGETHER"

SAFARNAMA is an educational and recreational trip organized by THRYVE, designed to provide students with an opportunity to explore wildlife, nature, and cultural attractions while enjoying a refreshing break from academic life. The trip includes a visit to Chhatbir Zoo, Chandigarh, where participants explore wildlife and learn about animal conservation, followed by Rock Garden and the tranquil Rose Garden, Chandigarh.
